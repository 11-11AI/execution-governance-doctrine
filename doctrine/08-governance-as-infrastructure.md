# 08 — Execution Governance as Infrastructure

**A mandatory layer for advanced AI.**

Identity, networking, and encryption became foundational layers of the
internet because they were infrastructure, not applications. Execution
Governance is the same kind of layer for AI: a control plane between AI
decisions and real-world effects, consumed by every system that cannot afford
to execute first and ask later — defense, finance, healthcare, critical
infrastructure.
